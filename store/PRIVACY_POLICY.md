# Privacy Policy — FuzeFrame

**Effective date: 16 September 2026**

FuzeFrame ("the app", "we", "us") is a camera app that captures a short burst of
RAW frames and merges them on your device into a higher-resolution photo. This
policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

## Summary

**FuzeFrame does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** Everything
the app does happens on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no
tracking, no advertising, and no third-party data collection.

## Data we collect

**None.** We do not collect personal information, usage analytics, device
identifiers, location, or contacts. The app has no servers of its own and does
not send your information anywhere.

## Camera

The app uses the camera **only** to capture the RAW frames you choose to shoot by
pressing the shutter. Frames are processed on your device and are never uploaded
or shared by the app.

## Photos

With your permission, the app saves the photos it produces to your device's photo
library and shows your captures in its in-app gallery. Your photos stay on your
device and under your control. The app does not upload, back up, or transmit your
photos. Any sharing is initiated by you, through the system share sheet or the
Photos app.

## Purchases

FuzeFrame offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove the free-capture limit.
Purchases are processed entirely by **Apple** through the App Store. The app does
not see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is
governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

To remember whether you have unlocked the purchase and how many free captures
remain, the app stores small non-personal values **locally on your device**
(in standard app storage). This information never leaves your device.

## Data sharing

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new
effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: **apps@spacetree.ventures**
