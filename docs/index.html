Multi-frame super-resolution camera — Support

FuzeFrame captures a short burst of RAW frames and merges them on your iPhone into a single higher-resolution, lower-noise photo, saved as a RAW DNG. This page has support info and answers to common questions.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email valentinkiryanski@icloud.com. We aim to reply within a few days.

Device requirements

The multi-frame merge is memory-intensive and requires an iPhone with at least 6 GB of RAM — iPhone 12 Pro, 13 Pro, and iPhone 14 or later. On devices with less RAM the app shows an “unsupported device” notice.

Frequently asked questions

Where do my photos go?

Finished photos are saved as RAW DNG files to your device’s photo library. Everything is processed on your iPhone — nothing is uploaded.

The app says my device isn’t supported.

Your iPhone has less than the 6 GB of RAM the merge needs. FuzeFrame requires iPhone 12 Pro / 13 Pro or iPhone 14 and later.

A capture failed or the app closed during processing.

The 48 MP merge uses a lot of memory. Close other apps and try again, or lower the number of frames in the capture controls.

How do I open the DNG files?

DNG is a standard RAW format. Open them in Photos, Adobe Lightroom, or any RAW-capable editor for full editing latitude.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases.
